Aegis Protocol is a game designed to detect and score recursive thinking as it occurs in real-time. 

The game takes place in an in-universe event where the Ship's Commander, Kristoff Briggs, is
trapped in his escape pod as the ship is on a collision course for a Monolith-class asteroid.

If Kristoff cannot convince the ship's Rogue AI to let him undock his escape pod he will die.

Can you do it?
