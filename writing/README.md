## Exegesis & Visual Listening

**Leather Terror – Visual Listening Notes**  
`Leather_Terror_Visual_Notes_BMSpangler.pdf`  
An excerpt from my ongoing exegesis of Carpenter Brut’s *Leather Terror*. Instead of treating the album as background music, I use specific track pairs – *Day Stalker / Night Prowler* and *Paradisi Gloria / Leather Terror* – as engines for rule-consistent combat and coronation simulations. The notes show how I map musical structure to in-head visuals, agency, and authored advantage.
