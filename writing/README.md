## How I use AI in my writing

I use AI as an editor and sparring partner, not as a ghostwriter. The ideas, structure, and voice start with me; the model helps tighten phrasing, spot weak lines, and pressure-test what I’m trying to say.

Most of the pieces here begin as fast, constraint-based drills that I write in real time. The AI proposes edits or alternate structures, and I accept or reject them line by line. The goal is simple: keep the moral stance and authorship mine, and use the tool to sharpen my thinking instead of letting it speak for me.

_For a longer description and concrete examples, see the accompanying PDF: `spangler_writing_drills_and_ai_collab.pdf`._
